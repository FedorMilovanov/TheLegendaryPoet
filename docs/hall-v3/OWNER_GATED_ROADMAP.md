After #403 there is no independently justified autonomous Product implementation transaction. Re-running the same rights searches, generating another offline exhibit, creating workflow-only churn or inventing a new Hall issue merely to keep work moving is forbidden.

A future bounded Hall transaction becomes selectable only when at least one material input exists:

1. explicit owner intended-use, final-credit and documentary-production disposition;
2. qualified legal or institutional evidence that materially changes a documentary rights record;
3. an actually submitted/fulfilled institutional copy request or an explicit owner decision to proceed without the optional Pushkin House drawing;

5. a newly reproduced engineering defect on the then-current Product `main`;

When such an input exists, open a **new bounded issue/lane for that concrete transaction**. Do not reopen #369 merely because the long-term museum roadmap continues.
