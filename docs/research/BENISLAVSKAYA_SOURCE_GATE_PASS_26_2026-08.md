## 1. Critical terminology correction

The proposed headline formula `35 letters, 14 replies` is not precise enough.

The academic name index to Yesenin's Complete Works, volume 6, states:

- 35 known **letters, notes and telegrams** from Yesenin to Galina Benislavskaya;
- 1 dedicatory inscription from Yesenin to Benislavskaya;
- 14 known letters from Benislavskaya to Yesenin.

Academic source:

- `https://feb-web.ru/feb/esenin/texts/es6/es6-754-.htm?cmd=p`

### Safe reader formula

> The surviving and published corpus includes 35 letters, notes and telegrams from Yesenin to Benislavskaya, one dedicatory inscription and 14 known letters from Benislavskaya to Yesenin.

### Unsafe formula

> `35 letters and 14 replies`

Why unsafe:

- it converts notes and telegrams into letters;
- it suggests every reverse letter is a direct reply to one of the 35 items;
- it hides the separate dedicatory inscription;
- it creates a false symmetric correspondence model.

## 2. Why popular counts conflict

A popular article by E. Naumov reproduced online states that fourteen Yesenin letters and ten Benislavskaya letters survived. This may reflect the smaller corpus available or selected in an earlier publication, not the final academic inventory.

Navigation source only:

- `https://esenin.ru/o-esenine/zhenshchiny-esenina/galina-benislavskaia/naumov-e-k-istorii-odnoi-druzhby`

The discrepancy must not be harmonised by guesswork. Use the academic PSS index for the total known corpus and identify the exact edition whenever discussing a smaller published selection.

## 4. Academic evidence already available without the missing scan

The FEБ academic corpus independently demonstrates Benislavskaya's editorial and agency role.

### Book composition and editorial decisions

The comments to volume 5 quote Benislavskaya's letter of 9 February 1925 about assembling material for a proposed volume, locating old journal poems, whether to include `Яр`, and how to arrange sections.

- `https://feb-web.ru/feb/esenin/texts/e75/e75-325-.htm?cmd=p`

### Publication planning and titles

The author-books register quotes correspondence about the planned title `После скандалов`, relations with `Круг`, placement of `Русь уходящая`, and editorial objections to duplicate naming.

- `https://feb-web.ru/feb/esenin/texts/es7/es7-0724.htm?cmd=2`

### Poem and book production

Academic comments to `Страна Негодяев` and related works quote Benislavskaya's reports on negotiations, submissions, planned combined editions, journals and refusals.

- `https://feb-web.ru/feb/esenin/texts/es3/es3-435-.htm?cmd=p`

### Legal and material authority

Comments to collective/business documents record her receiving a copy of a publishing document under power of attorney and identify the associated address and payment context.

- `https://feb-web.ru/feb/esenin/texts/e72/e72-266-.htm?cmd=p`

### Direct authorial instruction

The 29 October 1924 letter from Tiflis gives Benislavskaya instructions on:

- the composition of `Рябиновый костер`;
- corrections to `Русь советская`;
- inclusion and ordering of collections and poems;
- handling of publications and negotiations.

- `https://feb-web.ru/feb/esenin/texts/es6/es6-1832.htm?cmd=2`

The 20 December 1924 letter from Batum explicitly authorises her judgement in handling affairs while also revealing emotional dependence; neither layer should erase the other.

- `https://feb-web.ru/feb/esenin/texts/es6/es6-1912.htm?cmd=p`

## 5. Diary boundary

The online publication of diary fragments explicitly says:

- the autograph's location is unknown;
- the publication derives from a copy;
- the material is not a continuous conventional diary;
- it contains diary entries, copied letters and memoir-like drafts;
- later editors rearranged fragments chronologically and sometimes changed dating.

Navigation/publication page:

- `https://esenin.ru/o-esenine/vospominaniia/benislavskaia-g-dnevnik-fragmenty`

### Safe formula

> Benislavskaya's so-called diary survives through a published copy-based textual tradition; the autograph is not currently established, and the compiled material combines several genres.

### Forbidden formula

> `Benislavskaya wrote in her diary on [exact day]...`

unless the exact fragment, dating basis, copy provenance and editorial intervention are stated.
