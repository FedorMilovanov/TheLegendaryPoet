## 1. Source class

The book was written by Irma Duncan with Allan Ross Macdougall and published in 1929. It is an early connected memoir with access to Duncan's circle, not an immigration file, civil-registry act, passenger manifest or neutral official chronology.

Use it for:

- the Duncan-side narrative and origin of later formulas;
- named letters and quoted contemporary materials that can be traced independently;
- sequence comparison;
- identifying people, places and further archive targets.

Do not use it alone for:

- an exact date disputed by stronger academic chronology;
- legal motive or legal status;
- a medical diagnosis;
- a complete transcript of dialogue;
- the sole psychological explanation of marriage, travel or crisis.
