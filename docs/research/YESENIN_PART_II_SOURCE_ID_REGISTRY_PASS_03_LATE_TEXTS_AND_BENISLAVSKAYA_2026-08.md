## 6. New anti-regression rules

1. The cycle collection URL and the physical author book are different source objects.
2. A comment's general cycle chronology does not replace poem-specific manuscript evidence.
3. Posthumous first publication must not be described as lifetime publication.
4. A child URL is stronger than a sitemap placeholder but does not automatically close archive provenance.
5. A letter showing a plan to reach Tehran or Tabriz does not prove physical presence there.
6. Benislavskaya's editorial work is documented through exact instructions; it must not be reduced to the word `muse`.
7. Authorial praise of a work in a letter is dated self-evaluation, not an objective final ranking.
