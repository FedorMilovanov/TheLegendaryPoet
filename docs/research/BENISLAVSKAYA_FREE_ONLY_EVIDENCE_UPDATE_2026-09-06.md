## What this does — and does not — establish

The Perm 1995 and EUSP 2001 holder reports independently corroborate a later-edition sequence of **13 letters from 4 March 1924 through 4 May 1925**.

They do **not** prove that:

- Yushin's older 16 bibliography positions were fictional;
- the old 18 January / 8 February labels never referred to real texts;
- a simple arithmetic transformation converts 16 older positions into the later academic count of 14 letters;
- every omitted old date is a duplicate rather than a redating, reclassification, postcard/document-class issue, archive-description problem, or separate physical object;
- the later 13-letter sequence is itself a complete canonical description of all known Benislavskaya → Yesenin correspondence.

The project therefore continues to treat `13 open/later units ↔ 16 older bibliography positions ↔ 14 later academic letters` as an item-identity, dating, document-class and provenance reconciliation problem.
