## 1. Why poem-level IDs are required

The general `Персидские мотивы` cycle comment establishes composition phases and book history, but it cannot serve as the sole source for every poem-level statement.

Each selected poem needs its own:

- exact academic text URL;
- printed PSS pages;
- autograph/copy/publication evidence;
- date status;
- reader claim boundary.

The late-poetry chapter will use one early poem and at least one August 1925 poem to show that the cycle developed over time.

## 6. Textological anti-regressions

1. A cycle-level source does not replace poem-level source rows.
2. A poem's printed author date and manuscript evidence must be identified separately.
3. The late-May book is not the final complete cycle.
4. “Persian” characters may be poetic conventions rather than directly identifiable people.
5. A copied text with `Проверено С. Е.` is an authorised witness, not an autograph.
6. A posthumous first publication must be labelled posthumous.
7. Draft variants are evidence of work, not alternative lines to combine freely with the final text.
8. One poem can illuminate self-knowledge but cannot alone prove repentance, diagnosis or death intent.
