```md

Base / rollback: record exact current main and PR head before each mutation
Source of truth: current article data, typed Essay engine, source/rights ledgers and applicable validators
```

## Purpose

Complete and verify the current long-form article programme without multiplying branches, opening premature routes or mixing unrelated product work.

## Allowed

- editorial, historical-narrative and source-acquisition standards;
- durable owner invariants and anti-regression notes relevant to article production;
- source maps, claim matrices, chronologies and myth ledgers;
- Google Drive acquisition/deduplication policy and verified source queues;
- visual rights, provenance, caption and placement ledgers;
- approved cover replacement work when binary, metadata, provenance and pinned SHA update atomically;
- article data and universal Essay block improvements required by this marathon;
- related validators, exact-head checks and browser/mobile QA;
- publication queue and draft VK announcements that remain gated until production readiness;
- public editorial-policy clarification.

## Not allowed

- creating another branch or PR for the same editorial marathon;
- emblem work, which follows the narrow direct-main exception in root `AGENTS.md`;
- unrelated homepage, hall, music or community redesign;
- package/dependency upgrades unrelated to a proven marathon requirement;
- premature registration of source-gated articles;
- assigning a public date merely to fill the calendar;
- weakening source, provenance, cover, myth, citation, type or browser gates;
- replacing archive evidence with reconstruction;
- bulk-uploading PDFs merely to satisfy a search count;
- unrelated cleanup of old branches or files.

## Active work fronts

1. Sergey Yesenin, Part II: day-level chronology and claim matrix for 1921–1925.
2. Europe/America route: exact events, press, performances and the legal status of the US departure.

5. Non-duplicating VK announcement drafts for ready existing site articles.

## Collision rule

Continue all work above on this branch. A handoff changes owner, not branch. Out-of-scope findings go to an issue or ledger instead of a new speculative branch.

## Merge gate

- final diff remains within this scope;
- source, rights, content, type, build and applicable browser gates cover the final head;
- review threads are resolved without weakening contracts;
- no temporary writers/patchers/workflows survive;
- production is not claimed without same-SHA deploy/live evidence.
