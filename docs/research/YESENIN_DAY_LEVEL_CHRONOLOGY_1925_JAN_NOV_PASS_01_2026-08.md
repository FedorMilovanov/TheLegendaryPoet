## 1. Scope and governing boundary

This file deliberately stops on 30 November.

December 1925 requires a separate day-level source matrix because it combines:

- the continuing clinic stay;
- letters and visits;
- the departure from the clinic;
- the Moscow–Leningrad journey;
- hotel records and witnesses;
- the final poem's provenance;
- the initial inquiry and forensic documents.

Those events must not be compressed into the final rows of a broad annual chronology.

The January–November ledger has five major functions:

1. complete the Caucasus creative arc;
2. document the 1925 publishing/collected-works programme;
3. place Sofia Tolstaya in the chronology without the “saving marriage” legend;
4. separate the September train case from later events;
5. establish the November completion of `Черный человек` and the clinic admission without claiming the poem was simply “written in the clinic”.

## 2. Status vocabulary

- `DOCUMENTED` — primary letter, contract, legal/medical document, autograph, publication, poster or registry record.
- `ACADEMICALLY-ESTABLISHED` — academic commentary reconstructs the event from named evidence.
- `MEMOIR-MAPPED` — named recollection, retained with temporal distance and genre.
- `TEXT-DATED` — date supplied by autograph, authorised typescript or academic textual chronology.
- `PLANNED-NOT-REALIZED` — a documented book/event plan that did not reach completion.
- `DATE-RANGE` — secure interval without a fully pinned day.
- `HOLD` — insufficient for a final reader-level claim.

## 5. Key source distinctions

### `Анна Снегина`: completed poem versus unrealised book

```yaml
completed_text: January 1925, Batum
periodical_publication: documented in 1925
planned_separate_book: unrealised during Yesenin's life
```

Do not cite a planned standalone edition as a physical author book.

### `Персидские мотивы`: May book versus August final cycle

```yaml
first_six_poems: October 1924–January 1925
two_more: Moscow, March 1925
two_more: Baku, April 1925
book_publication: late May 1925
final_four_poems: Baku/Mardakan, August 1925
```

The late-May book is a real publication but not identical with the final complete cycle.

### Collected works: contract versus production outcome

```yaml
contract: 30 June 1925
manuscript_preparation: second half of 1925
proofs_read_by_yesenin: no
first_galleys: 31 December 1925
three_volumes_published: 1926
```

The 1925 contract and work are central to the biography; calling the set a 1925 published edition is false.

### `Черный человек`: long work history versus final revision

Safe formula:

> Yesenin said he had worked on the poem for roughly two years. The surviving final text was intensively revised on 12–14 November 1925 and completed on 14 November, before the clinic admission.

Forbidden formula:

> `Черный человек` was written in the psychiatric clinic in November 1925.

## 6. Sofia Tolstaya boundary

The evidence supports multiple simultaneous roles:

- partner and legal wife from 18 September;
- copyist/secretary for poems and collected works;
- preserver of manuscripts and dates;
- participant in household and medical decisions;
- later editor and archival witness.

The evidence does not support a simple formula that the marriage either “saved” or “destroyed” Yesenin.

Her copies are important textual witnesses, but each date must specify whether it is:

- authorial;
- supplied by Sofia from memory/knowledge;
- recorded during dictation;
- corrected by Yesenin;
- added later during collected-works preparation.

## 7. The September train case boundary

The case begins with the act of 6 September during the Baku–Moscow journey. It continues through the 29 October interrogation and November correspondence/intervention.

It is separate from:

- the September 1923 `Стойло Пегаса` case;
- the November 1923 four-poets case;
- clinic admission and the December final journey.

The certificate no. 1037 affects the ability to question him; it does not prove the underlying accusation or supply a diagnosis.

## 8. Standing anti-regression rules from Jan–Nov 1925

1. **Completed poem is not the same as a published standalone book.** `Анна Снегина` had an unrealised separate-edition plan.
2. **A book can precede the final cycle.** The May `Персидские мотивы` does not contain the full August-finalised corpus.
3. **A contract is not a published edition.** The collected works appeared in 1926.
4. **A power-of-attorney draft may be unfinished.** Do not upgrade every draft to executed authority.
5. **A spouse-copyist is not automatically the author of dates/variants.** Record the textual role precisely.
6. **Marriage registration does not prove emotional stability or spiritual recovery.**
7. **A train act begins a case; it is not a conviction.**
8. **An event poster does not prove attendance.** 29 November is another negative example.
9. **A final revision date is not the origin date.** `Черный человек` had a multi-year history.
10. **Clinic admission and certificate are not a complete medical chart.**
11. **Creative productivity does not prove recovery; illness does not make every poem a symptom.**
12. **December evidence remains separate.** Do not smuggle later events into the November interpretation.
