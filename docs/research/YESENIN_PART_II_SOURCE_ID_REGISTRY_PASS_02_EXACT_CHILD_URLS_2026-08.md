
### Reader use

- crafted retrospective address;
- guilt, historical self-interpretation and rhetorical self-justification;
- textual example of how a damaged typeface led many later editions to reproduce the wrong reading in line 41.

### Exact textual warning

The academic comments show that a defective printed `е` in some copies of `Страна советская` could look like `о`, producing the widespread later reading `Но зрело...` instead of the academically restored `Незрело...`.

The article must quote from the controlling academic text, not from a random modern webpage or popular edition.

### Boundary

- the poem is not a legal transcript of the marriage;
- addressee identification rests on later testimony and must be stated with attribution;
- confession in a poem does not by itself prove repaired relationship or repentance.
