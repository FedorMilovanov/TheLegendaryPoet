## 1. Chapter function

The chapter must prevent a false cinematic structure:

```text
arrival in Moscow
→ one quarrel
→ final break with Duncan
→ immediate tavern collapse
```

The documents support a more complex transition:

- return on 3 August;
- rapid writing and publication of `Железный Миргород`;
- public re-entry on 21 August;
- physical separation from Duncan and continuing correspondence;
- new relationship with Augusta Miklashevskaya;
- growing practical contact with Galina Benislavskaya;
- cafe economics and legal incidents developing alongside strong literary work.

## 2. Governing claims

- `YES2-C009` — Moscow return did not instantly end the Duncan relationship.
- `YES2-W005` — `Железный Миргород` is post-return prose, not an American diary.
- `YES2-R005` — `Любовь хулигана` is not a day-by-day relationship calendar.
- `YES2-I005`, `YES2-I006` — public role and real disorder must remain distinct.
- `YES2-L001` — late legal cases must not be fused.

## 3. Preferred opening

Open with the 14 August Moscow autograph of `Железный Миргород`, not the train/platform arrival.

**Reason:**

The autograph demonstrates what return meant intellectually: foreign experience was being converted almost immediately into crafted polemical prose.

**Possible lead:**

> Eleven days after returning to Moscow, Yesenin dated the autograph of `Железный Миргород`. The West had ended as a route, but not as a subject.

No direct quotation until the text and punctuation are page-checked.

**Safe formula:**

### Paragraph 8 — two legal lines

Compactly distinguish:

- 15 September cafe incident → 16 September protocol → 11 October criminal action → 23 November suspended hearing;
- 20–22 November separate four-poets case.

**Safe formula:**

> The files overlap in public memory but not in legal chronology.

**Claim ID:** `YES2-L001`.

### Paragraph 9 — work and disorder coexist

Required synthesis:

- strong prose and major lyric cycle;
- public readings and publishing;
- alcohol/cafe/legal incidents;
- continued relationships and practical dependence.

**Prohibited binary:**

- “he was recovering creatively”;
- “he had already ceased to work”.

### Paragraph 10 — transition to `Москва кабацкая`

Bridge:

> The Moscow return did not simply restore the old life. It converted the foreign journey into literature while giving new force to the role that would soon be fixed in the title `Москва кабацкая`.
