# Benislavskaya → Yesenin: RGALI 105 / 106 provenance resolution

Date: 2026-09-06  
Status: **EVIDENCE-WEIGHTED PROVENANCE RESOLUTION**  
Owner issue: `#200`  
Staging PR: `#425`

## Question

The early publication layer contains a conflict over the archival unit containing the typed-copy corpus of G. A. Benislavskaya's letters to S. A. Yesenin:

- E. I. Naumov (1970) cited `ЦГАЛИ, ф. 190, ед. хр. 106` for the ten typed-copy letters known to him;
- later witnesses repeatedly identify the Benislavskaya **letters** corpus as `ф. 190, оп. 1, ед. хр. 105`.

The purpose of this note is to decide which reference may control current project prose and provenance metadata without pretending that we have inspected the official unit-105 inventory card itself.

## Evidence hierarchy

### 1. Official archive fund identity — CLOSED

RGALI's official fund card confirms:

- fund **190**;
- `Есенин Сергей Александрович (1895–1925) — поэт`;
- personal-origin fund;
- 4 inventories / 456 storage units.

Public fund card:
`https://www.rgali.ru/fund/8211`

This confirms the archive/fund identity, but the currently exposed public search result does not independently display the title of op.1 unit 105. Therefore this note does **not** claim `OFFICIAL INVENTORY CARD VERIFIED` for unit 105.

### 2. Naumov 1970 — early conflicting citation: 106

E. I. Naumov, `К истории одной дружбы. С. Есенин и Г. Бениславская`, states that ten Benislavskaya→Yesenin letters were known to him in typed copies and cites:

`ЦГАЛИ, ф. 190, ед. хр. 106`.

Public transcription:
`https://esenin.ru/o-esenine/zhenshchiny-esenina/galina-benislavskaia/naumov-e-k-istorii-odnoi-druzhby`

This is important evidence for Naumov's source layer and must remain in the historical citation ledger. It is **not** treated as the controlling modern unit number because later independent evidence points consistently to 105 and the same Naumov layer already contains demonstrable date/citation instability elsewhere.

### 3. Royzman 1973 — direct corpus description: 105

M. D. Royzman, *Все, что помню о Есенине* (1973), explicitly distinguishes two Benislavskaya archival objects:

- `Воспоминания о Сергее Есенине` — `ЦГАЛИ, ф. 190, оп. 1, ед. хр. 122`, typescript;
- her `Письма` — `ЦГАЛИ, ф. 190, оп. 1, ед. хр. 105`, **typescript**.

Public text:
`https://esenin.ru/o-esenine/vospominaniia/roizman-m-vse-chto-pomniu-o-esenine/vse-stranitcy`

This is the strongest currently recovered explicit corpus-level statement because it names the document class (`Письма`), archive, fund, inventory, unit and carrier (`машинопись`) together.

### 4. Yusov 1994 — independent scholarly use of 105

N. G. Yusov, *Прижизненные издания С. А. Есенина: Библиографический справочник* (1994), cites Benislavskaya correspondence from:

`РГАЛИ, ф. 190, оп. 1, ед. хр. 105`.

The recovered OCR example is the 4 May 1925 Benislavskaya→Yesenin letter used to date the arrival of *О России и революции*.

Public OCR:
`https://imwerden.de/pdf/yusov_prizhiznennye_izdaniya_esenina_1994__ocr.pdf`

This is an independent later scholarly witness that the correspondence corpus was being cited as unit 105 after the archive's renaming from TsGALI to RGALI.

### 5. Zankovskaya 1997 citation chain — 105, leaves 27–29

A later secondary article reproduces the source locator attributed to L. V. Zankovskaya's 1997 publication of the 16 July 1925 Benislavskaya letter:

`ЦГАЛИ, ф. 190, оп. 1, ед. хр. 105, л. 27–29`.

Accessible secondary witness:
`https://spbspeaks.ru/2017/02/05/николай-астафьев-последняя-осень-сер/`

Because this is a later secondary relay rather than the archive inventory or Zankovskaya volume itself, it is **supporting triangulation**, not sole authority. It is nevertheless consistent with Royzman and Yusov and with the already independently page-verified existence of the 16 July text.

## Resolution

For current project provenance, use:

**`РГАЛИ (бывш. ЦГАЛИ), ф. 190, оп. 1, ед. хр. 105`**

as the **evidence-weighted controlling corpus reference** for the typed-copy collection of Benislavskaya→Yesenin letters.

Preserve `ед. хр. 106` only as:

**`EARLY NAUMOV 1970 CITATION / SUPERSEDED FOR CURRENT PROVENANCE`**.

Do not present 105 and 106 as equally probable alternatives.

## Why this is a resolution rather than an official-inventory claim

The evidence is asymmetric:

- one early publication says 106;
- a later memoir source explicitly calls the letters `ед. хр. 105, машинопись`;
- independent bibliographic scholarship cites the same correspondence corpus as 105;
- a separate later publication chain gives a specific 16-Jul leaf range inside 105;
- official RGALI confirms the parent fund 190, but an official public unit-105 title card has not yet been recovered in this pass.

Accordingly:

- **105 = controlling current reference**;
- **106 = historical citation discrepancy**;
- **official unit-title/card verification = desirable enrichment, not a reason to keep the two numbers co-equal**.

## Effect on the 13 ↔ 16 ↔ 14 reconciliation

This resolves only the **container/provenance conflict**. It does not by itself identify Yushin #121/#122/#128/#131/#134 as unique physical documents or settle their modern dates.
