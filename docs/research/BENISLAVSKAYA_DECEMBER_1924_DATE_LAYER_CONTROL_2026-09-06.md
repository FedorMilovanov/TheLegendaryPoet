## Scope

This note narrows two Yushin-1969 positions that remain problematic after the direct Ryazan holder readback of printed p. 411:

- **#128 — 30 Nov 1924**;
- **#131 — 29 Dec 1924**.

The purpose is not to force the old bibliography into the later 13/14-item corpus, but to separate **same-text dating**, **exchange chronology**, and **physical-document identity**.

## #128: 30 Nov → 1 Dec → 15 Dec date-layer conflict

### Yushin 1969 — physical old-date position

The Ryazan holder photograph of printed p. 411 directly verifies:

`128. Бениславская Г. А. Письмо С. А. Есенину от 30 ноября 1924 г.`

The page places the entry in Yushin's heading:

`Архивные документы, использованные или учтенные в книге`.

This proves the **old 30-Nov bibliographic position** was physically printed by Yushin. The entry itself does not reproduce the text and prints no item-level archive cipher.

### Naumov 1970 — same identifiable passage under 1 Dec

E. I. Naumov reproduces the passage:

`У меня тоже деловое настроение. Занимаюсь. Ваши стихи в порядок привожу, в Бедноте работаю...`

and labels it **1 December 1924**.

Public source:
`https://esenin.ru/o-esenine/zhenshchiny-esenina/galina-benislavskaia/naumov-e-k-istorii-odnoi-druzhby`

Naumov's source layer is especially useful here because elsewhere in the same corpus demonstrable date revisions/misdatings already exist. Its date labels therefore remain evidence about the early editorial layer, not automatic modern canonical dates.

### Later corpus / PSS — the same identifiable passage under 15 Dec

The later open Benislavskaya corpus places the same identifying passage inside the letter headed **15 December 1924**:

`У меня тоже деловое настроение. Занимаюсь, Ваши стихи в порядок привожу, в «Бедноте» работаю.`

Open corpus:
`https://esenin.ru/o-esenine/zhenshchiny-esenina/galina-benislavskaia/benislavskaia-g-pisma-k-eseninu`

The academic PSS commentary also quotes this passage from `Письма, 259` in the later 15-Dec context:
`https://predanie.ru/book/221933-tom-6-pisma/?chapter=chapter_446`

### Resolution for #128

Use the following model:

**`Yushin #128: 30 Nov` → `Naumov/1986 early layer: 1 Dec` → `later 1995/PSS/open corpus: 15 Dec`**

The **1-Dec and 15-Dec layers are text-identical at the decisive passage**. Yushin #128 occupies the corresponding old sequence slot under 30 Nov, but because the p.411 entry itself contains no incipit/text or item-level cipher, it is still too strong to claim that the physical #128 object has been independently proved identical to the later 15-Dec document.

Therefore current classification is:

**OLD #128 POSITION VERIFIED / SAME IDENTIFYING TEXT DIRECTLY PROVED ACROSS 1-DEC ↔ 15-DEC LAYERS / YUSHIN 30-NOV SLOT STRONGLY IMPLICATED IN THE SAME REDATING CHAIN / ITEM-LEVEL PHYSICAL IDENTITY STILL OPEN.**

Do **not** count 30 Nov, 1 Dec and 15 Dec as three letters merely because three date labels survive.

## #131: 29 Dec old position versus the controlled 25+27 Dec exchange

### Yushin 1969 — physical old-date position

The same holder page directly verifies:

`131. Бениславская Г. А. Письмо С. А. Есенину от 29 декабря 1924 г.`

So #131 is a real old bibliography position. It must not be called invented or absent from Yushin.

### Naumov 1970 — December correspondence remembered as three writes

Naumov says that in December 1924 Benislavskaya wrote to Yesenin **three times**, and in the passage used here he exposes/quotes the dates:

- **1 Dec**;
- **25 Dec**;
- **27 Dec**.

This early witness does **not** expose a separate 29-Dec item in the December sequence it discusses.

Public source:
`https://esenin.ru/o-esenine/zhenshchiny-esenina/galina-benislavskaia/naumov-e-k-istorii-odnoi-druzhby`

Because the same Naumov layer is demonstrably unstable in exact dating elsewhere, its absence of 29 Dec is not proof that #131 never existed. It is nevertheless relevant to the editorial-history conflict.

### Later PSS — response to Yesenin's 20-Dec letter is controlled at 25 Dec, with another letter on 27 Dec

Academic commentary explicitly says that Benislavskaya's answer to Yesenin's **20 December 1924** question about `Письмо к женщине` was contained in her **25 December** letter. It then independently cites her **27 December** letter as a further response/reaction.

PSS commentary:
`https://predanie.ru/book/221929-tom-2-stihotvoreniya-malenkie-poemy/?chapter=chapter_68`

The later open corpus likewise exposes distinct letters dated 25 Dec and 27 Dec:
`https://esenin.ru/o-esenine/zhenshchiny-esenina/galina-benislavskaia/benislavskaia-g-pisma-k-eseninu`

### Resolution for #131

A separate old Yushin **29-Dec position is physically verified**, but the later controlled exchange after Yesenin's 20-Dec letter is already represented by **25 Dec + 27 Dec**. The currently recovered Naumov and later-corpus witnesses do not expose a separate 29-Dec text.

Therefore current classification is:

**OLD #131 / 29-DEC POSITION VERIFIED / NOT NEEDED TO EXPLAIN THE CONTROLLED 20-DEC → 25+27-DEC EXCHANGE / TEXT AND PHYSICAL IDENTITY OPEN.**

Do not mechanically merge #131 into 27 Dec, and do not declare it nonexistent. The next useful evidence is item-level provenance/incipit/leaf mapping from the typed-copy corpus or a later editorial apparatus explaining the disposition of the old #131 position.

## Combined December implication

The three source layers are incompatible with any simple count arithmetic:

- Yushin old bibliography: 30 Nov, 25 Dec, 27 Dec, 29 Dec;
- Naumov discussion: 1 Dec, 25 Dec, 27 Dec, while saying Benislavskaya wrote three times in December;
- later corpus: between 10–12 Dec, 15 Dec, 25 Dec, 27 Dec.

This is direct evidence that the corpus underwent **dating and/or editorial-boundary revision**. A historical date label cannot be converted into a unique physical-letter count without object-level reconciliation.

## Publication boundary

Safe:

> The old Yushin bibliography preserves additional/earlier date labels, while later publication layers redistribute at least one identical textual passage and expose a different December sequence. The project therefore treats these as source-layer dating problems rather than multiplying letters by date labels.

Unsafe:

- `30 Nov, 1 Dec and 15 Dec are three separate letters`;
- `29 Dec is definitely the same letter as 27 Dec`;
- `Yushin #131 never existed`;
- `all December positions are now physically reconciled`.

No paid source route is required or authorized.
