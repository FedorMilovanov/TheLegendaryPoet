Date: 2026-08-19

This ledger separates **source layer**, **physical/document identity**, **document class**, **dating**, **page witness**, and **transmission state**. It must not turn a convenient numeric match into a canonical corpus.

## Controlling statements

1. The later academic PSS name index records **14 known letters** from G. A. Benislavskaya to S. A. Yesenin.
2. The open `Есенин.ру` publication contains **13 numbered units**, not 13 proved letters.
3. P. F. Yushin's 1969 bibliography contains **16 positions** dated 18 Jan 1924 through 16 Jul 1925.
4. The remaining controlling-book acquisition is `Сергей Есенин в стихах и жизни. Письма. Документы` (1995), printed pp. **236–281 inclusive**.
5. The 16 Jul 1925 text/page branch is already independently closed by IMLI 2013, pp. **339–340**.

The project therefore uses `13 ↔ 16 ↔ 14` only as a **reconciliation problem**, never as arithmetic proof.

## Critical document-class finding

The open publication's unit #4 is not merely a second item dated 26 April. The text explicitly says:

`Эта открытка вместо сдачи.`

Therefore **open numbered unit ≠ letter**. The open page contains an explicitly labelled postcard inside its 13-unit numbering.

This finding narrows the counting problem but does **not** solve it. A previously attractive shortcut — `13 units − 1 postcard + 1 Dec + 16 Jul = 14 letters` — is rejected because another independently attested letter text, the `Бросьте эту пьяную канитель` item dated 8 Feb in the earlier Naumov/Yushin layer, is also absent from the open 13-unit page.

If every outside-open candidate were physically distinct and belonged to the later PSS count, the working set would overshoot 14. At least one unresolved node must therefore still be explained by **redating, physical identity/duplication, editorial inclusion criteria, or document-boundary/classification**. Only the controlling pages may decide which.

## 16 July 1925 — page witness closed, transmission boundary open

The connected academic IMLI `Летопись`, vol. 5, book 1 (2013), pp. **339–340**, reproduces the 16 Jul letter continuously from its opening `Сергей...` through the signed ending `Галя. 16. VII. 25`.

The IMLI editors state that Zankovskaya's cited archive location is erroneous because the letter is absent there. They state that it **remained with G. A. Benislavskaya** and point upstream to the later publication/provenance literature.

Consequences:

- 16 Jul text/page witness = **VERIFIED**;
- do **not** attach the previously repeated `РГАЛИ, ф. 190, оп. 1, ед. хр. 105, л. 27–29` provenance;
- do not state that the letter was sent, delivered, received or read by Yesenin without a transmission witness;
- there is no need to purchase Zankovskaya 1997 p. 381 or Shubnikova-Guseva 2008 pp. 332–333 merely to obtain the text already reproduced by IMLI.

## What is proved before acquisition

- `13 numbered units` and `14 letters` are **different counting categories**.
- One open unit is explicitly a postcard.
- 1 Dec 1924 is an academically identified Benislavskaya→Yesenin letter outside the open 13-unit page.
- The `Бросьте эту пьяную канитель` text is independently academically attested as a Benislavskaya→Yesenin 1924 letter; earlier scholarship dates it 8 Feb.
- 16 Jul 1925 has a complete independent page witness.
- At least one remaining identity/date/classification node must still reconcile before a canonical 14-letter list can be asserted.

## What is NOT proved

Do **not** state any of the following before the controlling collation:

- that open 13 units plus/minus selected items already equal the canonical 14;
- that the 26 Apr postcard belongs to, or is excluded from, the PSS count solely because it is a postcard;
- that Yushin #128 and the academic 1 Dec letter are certainly the same physical document;
- that the 8 Feb letter is certainly distinct from every later-dated item;
- that 18 Jan, 29 Dec or 12 Apr positions are false;
- that a written item was necessarily sent/read;
- that every page anchor quoted in commentary equals the complete page span of the document.
