## Critical document-class finding

The open publication's unit #4 explicitly says:

`Эта открытка вместо сдачи.`

Therefore **open numbered unit ≠ letter**. The open page contains a postcard inside its 13-unit numbering.

A tempting shortcut — `13 units − 1 postcard + 1 Dec + 16 Jul = 14 letters` — remains rejected. The independently attested text beginning `Бросьте эту пьяную канитель`, assigned to 8 Feb in the older Naumov/Yushin layer, is also absent from the open 13-unit page and now conflicts with direct holder inspection of the later 1995 volume under that old date.

At least one unresolved node therefore still requires **redating, physical identity/duplication, editorial inclusion criteria, or document-boundary/classification**.

## 29 December 1924 — narrowed conflict, not closed

The late-academic topology now materially constrains Yushin #131:

1. Benislavskaya's verified/open 27-Dec letter belongs to pp. **263–264** in the later `Письма` layer.
2. From p. **265** the same late-academic apparatus already points to incoming correspondence from other correspondents.
3. Benislavskaya reappears securely at **20 Jan 1925**, pp. **268–269**.
4. Later academic reconstruction of the late-December exchange explicitly recognizes Benislavskaya replies on **25 and 27 Dec**, not a third separate 29-Dec reply.

This makes a separate late-PSS Benislavskaya letter on 29 Dec increasingly unlikely. It does **not** establish which earlier physical/textual unit Yushin #131 represented.

The open 27-Dec text also says that Benislavskaya planned to copy and sell the `Заря Востока` poems **on Monday**; 29 Dec 1924 was the following Monday. That calendar coincidence is recorded only as a **research lead about possible origin/context of the old date**, never as identity proof.

## 12 April 1925 — false mapping rejected

The previously tempting attempt to associate Yushin #134 with the passage about Flerovsky going to Persia is rejected. The open complete text places that passage inside the letter internally dated **`4/V-25`**.

The current April chronology is only negative/contextual: it documents surrounding outgoing Yesenin correspondence with Benislavskaya and other correspondents, but it does not itself prove whether a Benislavskaya→Yesenin object dated 12 Apr existed, was redated, or was omitted by later editors.

## 16 July 1925 — page witness closed, transmission boundary open

The academic IMLI `Летопись`, vol. 5, book 1 (2013), pp. **339–340**, reproduces the 16 Jul letter continuously from its opening `Сергей...` through the signed ending `Галя. 16. VII. 25`.

The editors state that Zankovskaya's cited archive location is erroneous because the letter is absent there. They state that it **remained with G. A. Benislavskaya**.

Consequences:

- 16 Jul text/page witness = **VERIFIED**;
- do **not** attach the previously repeated `РГАЛИ, ф. 190, оп. 1, ед. хр. 105, л. 27–29` provenance;

## What is proved before final reconciliation

- `13 numbered units` and `14 letters` are different counting categories.
- One open unit is explicitly a postcard.
- The inspected 1995 volume places 4 Mar on pp. 234–235, 6 Apr on p. 236 and 26 Apr on p. 238.
- The inspected 1995 volume apparently does not carry letters under the old dates 18 Jan and 8 Feb 1924.
- This absence-under-date does **not** prove the older-position texts nonexistent.
- Naumov's early layer independently keeps the 8-Feb and 6-Apr texts distinct; therefore they must not be collapsed merely to resolve the count.
- 1 Dec 1924 is an academically identified Benislavskaya→Yesenin letter outside the open 13-unit page.
- `Бросьте эту пьяную канитель` is academically attested as a Benislavskaya→Yesenin 1924 letter; its older 8-Feb dating/later-edition identity is unresolved.
- Yushin #131 / 29 Dec has a strong late-academic conflict and no obvious slot as a separate Benislavskaya letter in the later chronological page sequence.
- The Flerovsky/Persia passage belongs to the 4-May letter and cannot be used to close Yushin #134 / 12 Apr.
- 16 Jul 1925 has a complete independent page witness.
- At least one identity/date/classification node remains unresolved before a canonical 14-letter list can be asserted.

## What is NOT proved

Do **not** state before controlling collation:

- that open 13 units plus/minus selected items already equal the canonical 14;
- that the 26 Apr postcard belongs to or is excluded from the PSS count solely because it is a postcard;
- that Yushin #128 and the academic 1 Dec letter are certainly the same physical document;
- that the old 8-Feb attribution is simply false rather than redated/reidentified;
- that 8 Feb and 6 Apr are the same document;
- that 18 Jan, 29 Dec or 12 Apr positions are fictional/nonexistent;
- that Yushin #131 is certainly identical to the 27-Dec item merely because 29 Dec was the Monday mentioned in its text;
- that a written item was necessarily sent/read;
- that any single academic page anchor proves the complete span of a document.
