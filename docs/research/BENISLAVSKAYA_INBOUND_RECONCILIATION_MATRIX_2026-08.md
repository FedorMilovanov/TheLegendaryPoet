## Naumov 1970 typed-copy layer — text identity is stronger than its exact dates

Naumov states that he knew **10 Benislavskaya→Yesenin letters** in typed copies then held in `ЦГАЛИ, ф. 190, ед. хр. 106`. That statement is evidence about the corpus available to him, but the article's printed dates can no longer be transformed into a clean ten-row physical-document map.

Controlled examples:

- Naumov dates one late-April textual object **2 Apr 1924**; the same distinctive text is later published under heading **26 Apr 1924** with internal signature **`25.IV.24`**.
- Naumov separately treats the **8 Feb 1924** text and the **6 Apr 1924** text; therefore those two must not be merged merely to solve the count.
- A January 1925 textual object is referred to in the early layer as **1 Jan**, while the later complete document carries the internal date **`20.I.25`**.
- The paragraph beginning `У меня тоже деловое настроение. Занимаюсь. Ваши стихи в порядок привожу...` is assigned to **1 Dec 1924** by Naumov and repeated as such in the 1986 memoir commentary, while the later 1995/open/PSS layer places the same identifying paragraph inside the **15 Dec 1924** letter.
- Naumov also says Benislavskaya wrote to Yesenin in December `трижды`, while his article/date layer exposes a more complicated set of December references. That internal tension must not be silently harmonized.

Consequences:

- the reality of a textual object and the correctness of its old exact date are separate questions;
- the 8-Feb text remains real and separate from 6 Apr, but its exact old date is materially weaker as modern dating evidence than it would be in a stable publication layer;
- 18 Jan 1924, 29 Dec 1924 and 12 Apr 1925 are **not exposed as separate date-labelled objects in Naumov's article evidence used here** and remain `YUSHIN-EXTRA RELATIVE TO THIS NAUMOV WITNESS`;
- this does **not** prove those Yushin positions fictional: Yushin may have used additional sources, different copy labels or different editorial inclusion criteria;
- the 1-Dec label is no longer treated as an independent extra physical document: it is a **same-text date-layer conflict with later 15 Dec** until the Yushin #128 source note and physical identity are read.

This is an **article-level textual witness**, not a transcription of an archival inventory.
