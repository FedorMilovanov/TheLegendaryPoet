## 1. Why December is a separate matrix

The last month combines source types with radically different evidentiary value:

- primary letters and powers of attorney;
- a psychiatric-clinic certificate and missing/full medical records;
- authorised poem copies and autographs;
- contemporaneous meetings remembered in early memoirs;
- train/hotel/business documents that remain incompletely acquired;
- an original dated power of attorney in Leningrad;
- the final poem's manuscript and later laboratory study;
- the initial inspection, forensic act and photographs;
- later expert commissions and conspiracy literature.

A smooth narrative assembled from all of these without labels would be false precision. Every row therefore retains source class and unresolved boundaries.

## 2. Evidence hierarchy for the final-days chapter

Use in this order:

1. **Original contemporaneous document:** autograph, signed paper, register, official act, certificate, hotel/transport record, original photograph/negative.
2. **Academic publication of an identified original:** PSS, Chronicle, IMLI/RGALI/IRLI item with page/archive unit.
3. **Synchronous witness record:** statement or memoir written immediately/very soon after the event, still checked against documents.
4. **Early memoir:** named witness, date of writing/publication and access to the event disclosed.
5. **Late memoir:** useful only with distance/error warning.
6. **Later theory or reconstruction:** claim origin, source dependencies and missing proof must be explicit.

No later reconstruction may override a contemporary document merely because it is more dramatic.

## 4. Final poem: evidence card

### What is strong

- the physical poem/manuscript tradition is identified in the academic edition;
- Ustinova and Ehrlich independently place transfer on the morning of 27 December;
- both say it was written using blood because ink was unavailable;
- a 15 June 1992 microspectral laboratory conclusion identified the writing medium as blood;
- Ehrlich says he read it only on 28 December.

### What is not established by the poem itself

- a title supplied by the author;
- that it was explicitly addressed to Ehrlich in the text;
- that the author called it a farewell/suicide note;
- the complete psychological motive for writing it;
- that its literary speaker is a legal confession.

### Safe formula

> On 27 December Yesenin gave Ehrlich a short poem written in blood and asked him to read it later. The text became known only after the poet's death and was immediately interpreted as a farewell, but the manuscript itself does not label its legal or psychological function.

### Forbidden formulas

- `Yesenin left an unquestioned suicide note to Ehrlich.`
- `The poem alone proves the exact manner and motive of death.`
- `The blood story was invented later.`

## 5. Official version and alternative theories

### Required balanced formulation

The official investigation and forensic act concluded suicide. Later writers identified procedural gaps, inconsistencies or disputed interpretations. A gap is a legitimate research question; it is not positive proof of murder.

A 1991–1993 expert commission examined original photographs/negatives and medical materials. Its published conclusion stated that there were no objective materials then available that documented a refutation of the autopsy act and Gilyarevsky's 29 December conclusion.

### Evidence rule

For every alternative claim, record:

```yaml
claim:
first_known_origin:
contemporary_document_support:
physical_or_medical_support:
dependent_corroboration:

```

### Prohibited reasoning

- `The inquiry was imperfect, therefore murder is proven.`
- `The official version exists, therefore every later question is dishonest.`
- `A retouched published photograph is equivalent to the original negative.`
- `A literary reading of the final poem is a forensic conclusion.`

## 8. Standing anti-regression rules from December

1. **Clinic end-date is not the same as documented discharge mechanism.**
2. **Future plans do not disprove crisis; crisis does not erase future plans.**
3. **A memoir is not a hotel register.**
4. **The 27 December power of attorney is evidence of practical action, not a psychological verdict.**
5. **Written in blood does not automatically mean legally defined suicide note.**
6. **The recipient read the poem on 28 December, not necessarily at transfer.**
7. **Official version has documents; procedural criticism must address those exact documents.**
8. **An evidentiary gap is not positive homicide evidence.**
9. **Original negatives outrank retouched reproductions.**
10. **No graphic detail is included merely because a source contains it.**
11. **The last month cannot replace the previous four years as causal explanation.**
12. **Posthumous editorial/public myth begins immediately and must be separated from events before death.**
