## 5. Stable source rules

- Use one stable source object for one bibliographic item, even when several chapters cite it.
- A FEB sitemap is navigation, never the final article citation.
- Use exact child text/comment URLs where available.
- A physical book and its academic comment are different evidence objects.
- Internal research ledgers are planning authorities, not reader-facing historical sources.
- `PAGE-PENDING`, `BINARY-PENDING`, `RIGHTS-PENDING` and `HOLD` must remain visible until resolved.
- Do not convert a source into `VERIFIED-FOR-PUBLICATION` merely because its URL opens.
