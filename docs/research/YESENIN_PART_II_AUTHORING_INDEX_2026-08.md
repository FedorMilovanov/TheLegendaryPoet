## 7. Stable source rules

- One bibliographic item = one stable source object, reused across chapters.
- A FEB sitemap is navigation, never the final article citation.
- A physical book and academic comments are separate evidence objects.
- Internal research ledgers are planning authorities, not reader-facing historical sources.
- `PAGE-PENDING`, `BINARY-PENDING`, `RIGHTS-PENDING` and `HOLD` remain visible until resolved.
- A child URL pins text location but archive provenance must still be recorded where the academic comments provide it.
- Autograph, photostat, authorised copy, typescript and academic transcription are not interchangeable captions.
