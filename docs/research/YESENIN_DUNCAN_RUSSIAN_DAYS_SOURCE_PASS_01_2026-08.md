## 6. Research value and limits

This source directly addresses the Russian and later European years that `My Life` does not cover. It is important for:

- Duncan's Moscow school and Russian environment;
- the relationship and travel period involving Yesenin;
- chronology and memoir-origin tracing;
- comparison with letters, programmes, immigration documents and contemporary press.

It is not a neutral official chronology. The authors had personal access and perspective; every exact claim and quotation still needs a page-level citation and comparison with independent evidence.
