## 1. Method

A quotation enters the working chapter only when all are true:

- exact academic child page is pinned;
- printed PSS page is known;
- punctuation and line division were checked against the controlling text;
- source/provenance class is known;
- the fragment performs a necessary analytical function;
- the fragment is short enough not to replace the poem or independent analysis;
- it does not turn poetic speech into diagnosis, legal confession, biography transcript or proof of repentance.

Titles and opening lines already used as bibliographic identifiers are not counted as a reason to insert a second decorative quotation.

## 4. Orthography and punctuation decisions

- Use `Русь советская`, `Письмо к женщине`, `Персидские мотивы`, `Чёрный человек` as project titles.
- Preserve academic `Незрело`, not the widespread erroneous `Но зрело`.
- Preserve `октябрю` and `маю` in lowercase as in the academic text.
- Preserve the ellipsis and dash in `Я один... И — разбитое зеркало...`.
- Preserve `не бейся` with comma in the title/opening line.
- Exact-quotation rule: do not silently replace `ё`/`е` inside an exact quotation; the chapter's editorial prose may use project orthography outside quotations.

## 5. Theological and moral boundary

Approved fragments can support analysis of:

- estrangement;
- poetic autonomy;
- self-knowledge;
- the wish to stop searching;
- internal division;
- artistic self-accusation.

They cannot by themselves establish:

- a psychiatric diagnosis;
- a final political conversion;
- Christian faith or repentance;
- a repaired marriage;
- legal intent regarding death;
- the complete cause of the final crisis.
