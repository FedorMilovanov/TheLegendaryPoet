## 8. Moral/theological boundary

- artistic confession may reveal shame, self-knowledge and moral recognition;
- it does not by itself document repentance, reconciliation with God or durable changed fruit;
- religious imagery and talent do not establish conversion;
- the article may assess documented words and conduct but may not invent the unknowable final spiritual instant.
