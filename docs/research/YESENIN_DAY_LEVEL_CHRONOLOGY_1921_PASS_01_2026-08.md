## 2. Status vocabulary

- `DOCUMENTED` — dated primary document, publication object, poster or institutional record.
- `ACADEMICALLY-ESTABLISHED` — academic commentary reconstructs the event from named evidence.
- `DATE-RANGE` — event is secure, exact day is not.
- `ANNOUNCED-NOT-COMPLETED` — plan/document exists, outcome did not occur or is unproven.
- `MEMOIR-MAPPED` — evidence is memoir-based and must retain the witness name/date.
- `PROBABLE` — accepted scholarly date with an explicit qualification.
- `HOLD` — current evidence is insufficient for reader-level certainty.

## 5. Narrative consequences for Part II

### The opening chapter should not begin with Duncan

The chronology supports a stronger chapter structure:

1. book and group infrastructure in early 1921;
2. Yesenin's dated self-description on 26 February;
3. failed planned foreign travel;
4. Turkestan and `Пугачев`;
5. public readings and group disputes;
6. only then the probable meeting with Duncan.

This preserves causality: Duncan enters an already active, famous, institutionally embedded and internally unstable life.

### `Пугачев` is the year's main creative spine

The poem links:

- historical-route ambition;
- travel through the Volga/Ural/Turkestan corridor;
- private and public readings;
- theatrical interest;
- August completion;
- December physical publication;
- the wider question of how Yesenin's image-making worked beyond short lyric.

Part II should use it as the main bridge from group performance to the coming foreign journey, not treat 1921 as empty prelude.

### The group was not a single moral or aesthetic organism

The Block memorial conflict, Yesenin's own theory of “organic” image and the later Duncan transition all show internal difference. Avoid plural formulations such as `the Imagists believed...` unless a collective signed document is actually being discussed.

## 6. Anti-regression rules derived from this pass

1. **A poster proves announcement, not attendance.** The 17 October case must become the standing example in chronology work.
2. **Printed year is not always release date.** `Пугачов` says 1922 but appeared in December 1921.
3. **An autograph date may mark copying, not creation.** `Песнь о хлебе` was printed before the April 1921 autograph date.
4. **A travel petition does not prove travel.** The April foreign request failed; the Turkestan trip was a different journey.
5. **A memoir scene is not a transcript.** Keep witness, recording date and academic corroboration visible.
6. **A probable scholarly date must retain its qualifier.** The Duncan meeting is not upgraded from “apparently 3 October” to an unquestioned exact date.
7. **A group act does not automatically implicate every member.** The anti-Block evening must not be attributed to Yesenin merely because it was organised under the Imagist label.
8. **A plan is not a book.** `Ржаные кони` and `Эпоха Есенина и Мариенгофа` remain unrealised projects.
