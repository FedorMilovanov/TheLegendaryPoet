## 8. Important representation distinction

The October rows are printed from autographs held at IMLI. The December rows are printed from photostats of autographs held at IMLI.

Therefore visual captions must distinguish:

```text
autograph
≠ photostat of autograph
≠ academic transcription
≠ modern screenshot
```

A photostat may be strong textual evidence but must not be captioned as the original manuscript sheet.
