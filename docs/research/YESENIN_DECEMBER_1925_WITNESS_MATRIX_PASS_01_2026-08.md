## 1. Purpose

This matrix prevents the sentence `все вспоминали` from replacing named evidence. Each person has a separate row for direct observation, time of writing, first publication, dependence and unresolved contradictions.

Statuses:

- `PARTIAL-ROW` — some required fields are pinned; original/first text or full chronology remains incomplete.
- `PRESENCE-REPORTED` — presence is listed by another identified source; own account is not acquired.
- `DOCUMENT-WITNESS-PENDING` — official/hotel/medical person exists as part of a file, but the person/document row is not acquired.
- `HOLD` — no reader use beyond the safe formula.

No row in pass 01 is `COMPLETE`.

## 12. Dependency and contradiction rules

1. Ustinova and Ehrlich are separate early witnesses to the manuscript transfer; similarity does not make every detail independent.
2. Ehrlich’s 1926 and 1930 texts are two versions by one witness, not two witnesses.
3. G. F. Ustinov’s immediate article is strong for reception timing but must be separated from his role as acquaintance/hotel participant.
4. A person listed among visitors is not assigned a date, duration, dialogue or final-night role without their own or an independent source.
5. Later memoirs must disclose dependence on 1926 collections and newspaper publications.
6. Official documents are not witnesses in a literary sense, but their authors/signatories and page chains must be mapped individually.
7. No contradiction is resolved by choosing the more dramatic text.
