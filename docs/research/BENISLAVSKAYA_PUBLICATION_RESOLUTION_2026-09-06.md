## Decision

The former staging rule required a complete object-by-object reconciliation of the `13 ↔ 16 ↔ 14` correspondence layers before any reader publication. That rule was deliberately conservative while the Yushin records themselves were still unread.

The evidence position has materially changed. A direct holder photograph of printed p. 411 of P. F. Yushin, *Сергей Есенин: Идейно-творческая эволюция* (1969), now verifies the old archival-document sequence #121–#134, including the previously disputed labels 18 Jan, 8 Feb, 30 Nov, 29 Dec 1924 and 12 Apr 1925. Independent later academic/holder layers still expose a different 13/14-item horizon and directly demonstrated redating problems.

The publication gate is therefore narrowed from **complete corpus identity** to **claim-level evidentiary sufficiency**.

This is not a declaration that the unresolved old positions have been solved. It is a decision that the reader article does not need to solve them in order to make its actual claims.

## Reader claims that are publication-ready

The article's substantive claims rely on stronger direct or academic evidence independent of a complete 14-letter physical map:

- Benislavskaya's practical role in manuscript, correspondence and publishing logistics;
- Yesenin's 12 Jun 1924 power of attorney and the resulting Gosizdat contract;
- Yesenin's own manuscript, correction, publishing and payment instructions;
- the coexistence of domestic, family and literary-work responsibilities;
- the later academic count of 14 known Benislavskaya → Yesenin letters;
- the open publication's 13 numbered units, including a unit explicitly identifying itself as a postcard;
- Yushin's older archival-bibliographic sequence as a distinct historical layer;
- the independently page-verified 16 Jul 1925 text in IMLI 2013, pp. 339–340;
- the distinction between evidence of practical delegated work and an unsupported modern occupational title such as universal literary agent/editor.

None of those reader claims requires the project to assert that Yushin #121, #128, #131 or #134 is a separately identified modern-canonical letter.

## Uncertainty that remains explicit after publication

The reader publication must continue to preserve these boundaries:

- `13 numbered/open units ↔ 16 older Yushin positions ↔ 14 later academic letters` is not arithmetic proof of a physical corpus;
- #121 / 18 Jan 1924 remains without a recovered public incipit/object-level identity;
- #122 / 8 Feb has an independently real text in the early layer, but the exact old date remains unstable as modern dating evidence;
- #128 remains in a 30 Nov / 1 Dec / 15 Dec date-layer conflict;
- #131 / 29 Dec is physically verified as a Yushin record but is not needed for the later controlled 25+27 Dec exchange model;
- #134 / 12 Apr is physically verified as a Yushin record but conflicts with the later chronology in which the response to Yesenin's 8-Apr letter was written only on 4 May;
- the archive-container reference is evidence-weighted toward `РГАЛИ (бывш. ЦГАЛИ), ф.190, оп.1, ед.хр.105`; Naumov's `106` citation remains a historical discrepancy, not an equal current alternative;
- the 16-Jul letter is page-verified, but sending/delivery/reading by Yesenin is not asserted.

The public text must not convert any of these unresolved identities into a positive factual claim.

## Why a complete paid/closed witness is no longer a prerequisite

The previous 1995 continuous-page acquisition requirement was a research strategy for reconstructing the corpus, not a reader claim in itself. Direct free holder evidence has since established the old Yushin layer, later holder reads establish the 13-letter editorial horizon, and academic evidence controls the specific substantive episodes used by the article.

A paid scan, paid EDD, paid thematic search, paid archive copy or paid rights acquisition would not be proportionate merely to erase an uncertainty that the final article can state accurately.

**No paid acquisition is authorized or required for v1 publication.**

If future free evidence identifies the remaining old objects, that is an enrichment/update lane, not a retroactive prerequisite for the claims admitted here.
