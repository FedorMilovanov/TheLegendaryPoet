# Owner Invariants — THE LEGENDARY POET

**Effective:** 2 August 2026  
**Authority:** direct owner instructions and `PROJECT_CHARTER.md`

This file stores durable owner decisions, not a snapshot of one chat, sandbox or branch.

## 1. Authority

When instructions conflict:

1. current direct owner decision;
2. this file, root `AGENTS.md` and `PROJECT_CHARTER.md`;
3. current `main`, relevant active PRs/issues and exact-head checks;
4. current source registries, validators and policies;
5. historical reports and old branches as evidence only.

## 2. Truth and editorial judgment

1. No plausible falsehood. A smooth sentence, emotional arc or attractive caption never substitutes for evidence.
2. A technical change does not refresh `dateModified`, sitemap freshness or public editorial claims.
3. A poem, quotation, date, number, title, attribution and myth verdict must be traceable to a concrete source.
4. Primary documents and academic editions outrank popular retellings; a primary witness may still be mistaken or self-serving and must be read critically.
5. A reconstruction is never historical evidence and must be labelled as reconstruction.
6. Religious imagery does not prove personal Christian faith, repentance or salvation.
7. Moral clarity does not permit invented motives, diagnoses or final spiritual judgments.
8. A disputed claim remains qualified or `HOLD`; uncertainty is not a defect to hide.
9. Myths are corrected without sensationalism: identify the repeated formula, its origin, the documentary answer and the limits of the verdict.
10. The governing formula remains: **accuracy without whitewashing, directness without sensationalism, compassion without sentimental falsehood**.

## 3. Architecture and visual integrity

11. New articles use the universal essay engine. Do not build route-specific trains of custom pages and components when existing typed blocks are sufficient.
12. A new block type requires a typed data contract, renderer support, exhaustive handling and validation.
13. No weak placeholders, unfinished gimmicks or arbitrary visual clutter are shipped.
14. Owner-approved images and the canonical emblem are not replaced merely because an agent prefers another style.
15. Archive image, restoration and editorial reconstruction must remain visibly and structurally distinct.
16. Cover binary, alt, credit, source URL, provenance, `coverKind` and pinned SHA change atomically.
17. Visual quality is proven through real desktop/mobile inspection, not by green CI alone.

## 4. Process and branches

18. Do not multiply branches. One active independently mergeable lane has one canonical branch and PR.
19. The long-form editorial marathon continues in `editorial/longform-marathon-2026-08`, PR #271.
20. The direct-main emblem workflow is a narrow explicit exception and is not precedent for editorial or research work.
21. Never overwrite, reset, rebase, force-push or delete another agent's active work.
22. Findings outside the current scope are recorded and routed; they are not silently mixed into a large PR.
23. Final checks apply to final head. Old green evidence does not move with the branch.
24. Source merge, built artifact and production/live state are three separate boundaries.
25. Important decisions, failures, anti-patterns and “how not to do it” must be written into durable repository or Drive documentation.

## 5. Google Drive and source archive

26. Search broadly, acquire narrowly. A 40+ search marathon creates candidates, not forty automatic uploads.
27. Before upload, deduplicate against every existing library, batch, manifest and checksum registry.
28. Add only material that closes a named gap in an active or approved article/research plan.
29. Store one canonical master copy. Use dated immutable snapshots only when historical preservation is needed.
30. Never create uncontrolled `FINAL`, `LATEST`, `ULTIMATE`, `NEW`, `COPY`, `(1)` or similar duplicates.
31. Every accepted PDF receives provenance, bibliographic identity, rights status, page count, size, text-layer status and SHA-256.
32. Pirated, rights-unclear, corrupted, misleading, redundant or low-value files are not uploaded.
33. Public-domain status of a book does not automatically establish reusable rights for every embedded photograph or later editorial layer.
34. Source files, rights/manifests, research notes and publication exports remain in their separate Drive areas.

## 6. Publication queue

35. The site carries the complete article; VK receives a concise independent announcement and link, not a duplicate long article.
36. Already scheduled posts are not recreated or repeated.
37. Proposed dates remain proposals until article route, cover/OG, source, visual and browser gates pass.
38. A post does not promise archival richness, certainty or a final solution that the underlying article does not possess.
39. Postflight requires correct group/date/timezone, preview image, link, duplicate check, saved post ID and no blind retry after an unknown API result.

## 7. Definition of done

A material is ready only when fact, source, rights, structure, visuals, metadata, mobile/browser behaviour and publication claims agree with one another on the same final state.
